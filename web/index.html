01 · stream

Clean and preintegrate

The CSV is read in 2048-row chunks. Dead, stuck and spike samples are dropped; the rest is compressed into 20 Hz preintegration nodes.

02 · solve

Fit the drift, don't filter it

Drift is the double integral of a few sensor errors and is linear in them. One least-squares solve under a bounded-motion prior removes it.

03 · physics

Rotor drag as a speedometer

Sideways to the thrust, a multirotor's accelerometer feels only drag: f = −μ v + c. μ is calibrated from each file and switched off when absent.

04 · output

A pose for every row

Each dropout over 0.3 s gets its own unknowns. The final 5 s are never trusted: the pose is held, the safest policy we measured.
